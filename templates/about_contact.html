About Me

I'm Washim — a Computer Science Engineering student who got hooked on building things early and never really stopped. What started with simple scripts grew into full-stack applications, machine learning pipelines, and intelligent systems that take on real problems. I work across Python, Java, and the web stack, but what drives me isn't the language — it's the outcome.

Over the past couple of years, I've shipped projects spanning a wide range of domains. AgriTrade, my major project, connects farmers directly with buyers through an e-auction platform — cutting out middlemen and giving farmers more control over their income. I've also built a Fraud Detection System, a Driver Drowsiness Detection model, a Brain Tumor Classifier, and FinTrackAI — a personal finance tracker with smart categorization. Each project taught me something different, and each one had to work in the real world, not just on paper.

I've completed internships in AI with Python, Machine Learning, Full Stack Development, and currently AWS — four different domains that have sharpened how I think about software end-to-end. I don't just build features; I think about the user, the system, and the impact. If you're working on something meaningful and need someone who shows up ready to contribute from day one, let's talk.
